App use:
-------
"androidSlagalica" is an Android app, copying a popular gameshow / quiz called "Slagalica", where paired up players get to test their knowledge against one another. 
A single 1-on-1 match is a timed score-based PvP challenge, including several games in a sequence, which are "Who knows - knows!", "Connect two", "Associations", "Jumper", "Step by step" and "My Number".

One's own profile keeps a statistic, is customizable;
Existing player profiles are searchable;
Challenge a friend to a match or queue up to be met by a random opponent;
Enjoy your favorite time-limited games, keep improving under pressure and expanding your knowledge & be recognized as the top-ranked player on the auto-updating placement lists.
